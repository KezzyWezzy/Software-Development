Cricket Chalk

20 down to 15 & bull

Players Rules
—

How to keep score. Tap a number in the throwing player's column to chalk a mark — pick Double or Treble first and one tap chalks two or three.

  • / ✕ ⊗ — one, two, three marks. Three marks closes the number for that player.
  • Once you've closed a number, further hits score its face value (bull = 25) until every opponent closes it too.
  • Standard: the points go to you. Cut-throat: they go onto every opponent still open on that number — low score wins.
  • Win by closing all seven numbers with a score that beats the field.
