What we sell, by operator type

Four offers

Full coverage Tier A

10–100 miles, small system overall

  • LDS on the whole line plus 1–7 solar docks
  • The drone patrols the whole right-of-way and checks every LDS alarm
  • Replaces aircraft patrol

HCA segments + alarm checks Tier B

Over 100 miles

  • Docks only at river crossings, towns and marsh
  • An LDS alarm sends the drone; live video in about 10 minutes
  • Sell on avoided false-alarm shutdowns

LDS only Tier C

Under 10 miles, refinery and terminal lines

  • No drone needed inside the plant
  • Small deals that close fast
  • Refiners refer each other

Marsh & offshore Lead story

Louisiana coast and Gulf gathering

  • Trucks can't reach the line; boats and marsh buggies are slow
  • Sheen on water is easy to see from the air
  • Nearly every mile is HCA

First 90 days

Timeline

WEEKS 1–2

Prepare and open with Genesis

  • Request a Genesis meeting and propose a pilot on one Louisiana segment
  • Build the one-page pitch and a demo: an LDS alarm automatically sends a drone (simulator)
  • Re-run the tool for Texas (--states TX) to get correct HCA miles
  • Find the control room and integrity managers for Waves 1–2
WEEKS 3–6

Waves 1 and 2

  • Offer: a 90-day pilot with 1 dock and the LDS on a 10–15 mile HCA segment
  • Pilot success measures: time to confirm an alarm visually, false-alarm shutdowns avoided, patrol cost replaced
  • Start the FAA beyond-line-of-sight approval now. It takes the longest of anything here
WEEKS 6–10

Wave 3 and refinery short lines

  • Texas independents: full-coverage proposals
  • Refineries: LDS-only quotes, pursued in parallel
WEEKS 8–13

Wave 4 and pilot install

  • Multi-entity proposals sized to HCA segments
  • Install the first pilot dock, then publish a case study with real alarm-response times
Planning numbers (estimates)
  • Dock with sensors and solar: about $60–90k each
  • Running cost: about $15–30k per dock per year
  • One dock covers about 15 miles of line
  • Value of an alarm check: a 100,000 bbl/day line loses about $10–30k for every hour it's shut down
Data notes
  • Source: PHMSA annual report data (2025 report year), mileage from the per-state breakdown parts
  • Louisiana figures use the corrected HCA source (Part B). Texas HCA figures came from an earlier run that undercounted, so re-run before ranking Texas by HCA
  • Gas mileage includes regulated gathering lines
  • "Docks" assumes full coverage. An HCA-only plan needs fewer
