Bundle rule

10% off the LDS one-time fee when the customer buys two or more docks at the same time. It nudges hybrid deals without discounting the docks, which carry the thinner margin.

Our dock costs about 3–5 times a bare DJI or Skydio unit. The difference is the solar power system, the methane sensor, install, and the link that launches it on an LDS alarm. Expect to defend that in every hybrid deal; the 90-day pilot is how you prove it.

Before quoting customers
  • Replace the dock cost with real supplier quotes (Skydio, a methane payload, a solar integrator)
  • Get one competing CPM quote (Pipewise or DNV) for a 20–50 mile line to confirm the Standard price
  • Check whether DJI equipment is acceptable to your customers before choosing hardware
